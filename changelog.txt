Version: 2.1.0
Date: 2026-09-07
  Other:
    - Update for Factorio 2.1
  Bugfixes:
    - Stop crashing on load: 2.0 renamed the table a mod's state lives in from global to
      storage, and this mod stores the day counter there
    - Stop crashing anywhere south of the equator, and at the poles. Dawn and dusk now
      lengthen with distance from the equator in both hemispheres, where a southern
      latitude used to drive their length negative and put evening before dusk; and a
      polar winter now keeps a sliver of daylight rather than none, which used to put
      dusk below zero. Either one had the engine refuse the whole set of times and take
      the mod down with it. Latitudes 0 to 68 are unaffected.
